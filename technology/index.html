Recognition

A character model built on ConvNeXt-Small (≈ 49.5 M parameters) reads single characters at ≈ 99.85 % on clean and ≈ 99.65 % on scan-like characters in our internal test. The next stage reads whole text lines; preliminary line accuracy is about 93–94 %.

Document structure

A document is more than text: columns, headings, blocks and the relations between them. For résumés we map this structure to a schema of 234 fields in 21 blocks.

Processing and editing

Editing a PDF means changing content while the typography, layout and graphics stay as they were. PDFit uses the fonts embedded in the document; RESUMagic, in development, adds an understanding of résumé sections and blocks.

AURORA PDFit →

Training and evaluation

Models are trained with hard-example mining, targeted retraining and simulated scanner and camera damage. They are tested on held-out internal data and on public benchmarks, olmOCR-bench and UNLV.
