About

Practical intelligence for complex documents

AURORA Ai.Lab is a technology brand of Sistema de Autorecluta, S.L., based in Spain. We build products and our own methods for document recognition, structured extraction, PDF editing and document-focused machine learning.

Technology

Why documents are difficult

  • Hundreds of thousands of fonts
  • Complex layouts
  • Scans and phone photos
  • Several alphabets
  • Structure hidden in the design

The same letter looks different in every font, and the same document looks different after every scan. Look-alike characters such as l, I and 1 or o and 0 decide whether a phone number or an amount is right. That is why we start from the character.

How we work

  1. ResearchStudy the problem and published methods.
  2. DataCollect and check the data ourselves.
  3. Models and methodsTrain, then test on data never used in training.
  4. ProductsTurn what works into useful tools.
