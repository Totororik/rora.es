Questions

Does it keep the original font?

In most cases, yes. Many PDFs carry only the letters that were used. PDFit recognises the font and takes the full version from our collection of {{ site.data.metrics.fonts.value.en }} fonts, instead of a substitute such as Arial or Times.

Do I need to install anything?

No. PDFit works in your browser.
