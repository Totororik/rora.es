AURORA ML Training Datasets and model training for document AI

We built our own font data and training pipeline to teach AURORA OCR to read. Now we offer them to teams that train their own document models.

What we offer

Model training

We train or fine-tune a model for your task: characters, text lines, fonts or page layout. Hard-example mining, targeted retraining and simulated scanner and camera damage are built in.

Datasets

Access to our font and glyph data: hundreds of thousands of fonts, each split into letters and digits that are checked and labelled.

Independent evaluation

We test your model on our internal test sets and on public benchmarks, olmOCR-bench and UNLV, next to 7–8 established PDF readers and OCR tools.

The data behind it

219,171unique fonts, consolidated from 374,936 font files
13.3 Mcharacter fingerprints
≈ 13.8 Mvalidated glyphs: 11.7 M letters, 1.9 M digits and 0.2 M other signs, checked and labelled
110Latin and Cyrillic letters and digits in the current model

How it will work

One platform with API and SDK access to datasets, training resources, document-processing methods and model evaluation. The platform is in development, and we are taking early-access requests now.
