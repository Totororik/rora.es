What makes it different

Our know-how

Frames grow with your text

Add a line inside a box, and its frame and background stretch to fit. Ordinary PDF editors leave the box as it is, and the text runs over it.

Fonts

The original font

New words are typed in the PDF's own font, so nobody can see where you made the change.

Layout

Everything else stays put

Columns, lines, icons and photos keep their places; the text below moves down neatly.
