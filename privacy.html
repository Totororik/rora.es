What data we process

When you write to us

If you email info@rora.es or vladimir@rora.es, we use your address and the content of your message only to reply and to continue the conversation you started. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering, or steps before a contract at your request (Art. 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep the correspondence while it is relevant and then delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

When you visit the site

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub records the visitor's IP address in server logs for security and operation of the service.
  • Fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.), so your browser sends your IP address to Google.

Both providers take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We do not receive or combine this data ourselves.

Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies. The site saves only your language choice (EN/ES) in your browser's local storage, so the page opens in the same language next time. It never leaves your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.
