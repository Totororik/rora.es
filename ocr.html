How to read these numbers. They are character-level and text-line results, not full-page or end-to-end document OCR accuracy. Upper- and lower-case letters that share one shape (c/C, o/O, s/S and similar) are scored as correct by the character model; their case is decided afterwards by a separate rule from the geometry of the text line.

How we evaluate

We test on independent public benchmarks, not only on our own data: olmOCR-bench (real PDFs) and UNLV (scanned business documents). Our methods are compared with 7–8 established PDF readers and OCR tools, category by category:

  • Small print
  • Scan quality
  • Tables
  • Formulas
  • Multi-column pages
  • Headers and footers

Benchmark results are being prepared for publication.

What we are working on

Reaching at least 99.8 % under realistic degradation: displaced characters, background artefacts, blur, low contrast, varying stroke weight and phone photos of paper. The training pipeline uses hard-example mining, targeted retraining and simulated scanner and camera damage.

The next stage is line-level OCR: preliminary text-line accuracy is about 93–94 %.
