How accuracy is counted. Upper- and lower-case letters that share one shape (c/C, o/O, s/S and similar) are scored as correct by the character model. Their case is decided afterwards by a separate rule from the geometry of the text line.

How we measure

We test on independent public benchmarks, not only on our own data: olmOCR-bench (thousands of real PDFs with tables, formulas, headers and small print) and UNLV (scanned business documents). Our processing methods are compared against 7–8 established PDF readers and OCR tools.

Benchmark results will be published here.

What we are working on

Reaching at least 99.8 % under realistic degradation: displaced characters, background artefacts, blur, low contrast, varying stroke weight and phone photos of paper. The training pipeline uses hard-example mining, targeted retraining and simulated scanner and camera damage.

The next stage is line-level OCR: preliminary text-line accuracy is about 93–94 %.
